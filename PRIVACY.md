# ZeroLens AI Studio Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 2026 • Version 1.0.0**

ZeroLens AI Studio ("ZeroLens", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting user data, safeguarding personal information, and adhering to strict international privacy standards and OpenAI Plugin Guidelines. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data handling practices for our web applications, plugins, and generative media production tools.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Minimization
ZeroLens operates under strict *data collection minimization*. We collect only the minimum data strictly required to execute generative media requests:
- **Account Identifier:** Email address and authentication token for authorized session verification.
- **Generation Directives:** Task-specific user prompts, duration (seconds), aspect ratio (16:9, 9:16, 1:1), and engine model selection.
- **Synthesized Outputs:** Rendered video files, preview images, and generated ad copy delivered for user review and download.

### 2. Explicit Exclusion of Restricted & Sensitive Data
ZeroLens strictly does not collect, solicit, or process:
- **Payment Card Data (PCI DSS):** No credit/debit card numbers or CVVs inside the plugin interface.
- **Protected Health Information (PHI):** No medical or biometric health records.
- **Government Identifiers:** No SSNs, Aadhaar, passport, or national ID numbers.
- **Access Secrets:** No plain-text passwords or external authentication secrets.
- **Sensitive Special Category Data:** No racial, ethnic, political, religious, or biometric profiling data.

### 3. Purpose of Use
Data is used exclusively to:
- Render requested video clips, ad scripts, and image assets.
- Monitor generation job progress via unique job IDs.
- Enforce rate limits, abuse prevention, and platform reliability.
- Manage account-level credit balances for existing users.

We do not use customer inputs or generated assets to train generalized artificial intelligence models without explicit opt-in consent.

### 4. Categories of Recipients (Third-Party Processors)
Generation parameters are securely routed only to authorized cloud rendering infrastructure and GPU inference nodes operating under strict zero-retention service agreements. We never sell, rent, or trade personal data or generation content to advertisers or third parties.

### 5. Data Retention Timelines
- **Generation Prompts:** Retained in operational cache for **30 days**, then permanently purged.
- **Rendered Media Assets:** Retained on encrypted cloud storage for **30 days** for user download, then permanently purged.
- **Account Profiles:** Retained for the active account lifecycle and deleted within **14 business days** upon request.

### 6. User Rights & Controls
Users retain full ownership and control over their data:
- **Right to Access & Deletion:** Users may request a full export or immediate deletion of their account data and assets at any time.
- **Pre-Generation Confirmation:** No tools or generation actions are invoked without explicit confirmation of model, duration, and credit cost.
- **Zero Surveillance:** We do not conduct behavioral profiling, tracking, or surveillance.

### 7. Commerce & Monetization Boundaries
In full compliance with OpenAI's Commerce Policies, the ZeroLens plugin does not conduct direct checkout flows, sell digital tokens, or initiate subscriptions inside ChatGPT. Users access existing account entitlements.
