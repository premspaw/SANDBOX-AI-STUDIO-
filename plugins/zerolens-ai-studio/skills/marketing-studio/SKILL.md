# Marketing Studio Workflow

This skill handles multi-channel marketing campaigns, social media carousels, brand voice management, and promotional visuals.

---

## 1. Multi-Slide Social Carousels (`marketing_generate_carousel`)

Social carousels drive strong engagement and bookmark rates. The skill structures them across 5 to 10 slides:

- **Slide 1 (Hero Hook)**: Bold single-line statement + high-contrast visual focus. Must compel a right-swipe.
- **Slide 2 (The Problem / Context)**: Validate the audience's common misconception or pain point.
- **Slides 3–5 (Actionable Steps / Framework)**: 1 core insight per slide with visual diagrams or clear typography.
- **Slide 6 (Summary / Cheat Sheet)**: Digestible overview that encourages users to save the post.
- **Final Slide (Call to Action)**: "Save for later", "Share with a colleague", or link to product.

Default aspect ratio: `4:5` (vertical portrait) or `1:1` (square).

---

## 2. Brand Voice Cloning (`marketing_clone_brand_voice`)

Extracts the tone, rhythm, vocabulary, and identity markers from existing brand writing:
- Analyzes sentence length, emotional charge, humor level, and technical depth.
- Produces a reusable **Voice Manifest** to ensure all generated ads, carousels, and video prompts match the brand's exact personality.

---

## 3. Product Launch Campaign Kits (`marketing_create_full_campaign`)

Generates a unified launch toolkit for any product:
1. **3 High-Intent Ad Angles**: Problem-solution, aspirational, and comparison vs alternative.
2. **Video Ad Prompts**: Structured for Seedance 2.5 and Omni 1.1 Flash.
3. **Carousel Plan**: 7-slide educational authority carousel.
4. **Email / Community Announcement Copy**: Hook, body, and CTA.

---

## 4. Handwritten Menus & Aesthetic Chalkboards (`marketing_create_handwritten_menu`)

Specialized creative tool for cafes, restaurants, bakeries, and boutique food brands:
- Generates aesthetic chalkboard menus, kraft paper recipe journals, and artisanal typography posters.
- Accurately integrates dishes, prices, ingredients, and uploaded dish photos.
