ZeroLens AI Studio Privacy Policy

ZeroLens AI Studio ("ZeroLens", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting user data, safeguarding personal information, and adhering to strict international privacy standards and OpenAI Plugin Guidelines. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data handling practices for our web applications, plugins, and generative media production tools.

Core Privacy Commitment: ZeroLens operates under strict data collection minimization. We never engage in surveillance, behavioral profiling, or sell user information. We do not process sensitive personal data or financial credentials.

1. Data Collection & Minimization

In accordance with collection minimization principles, ZeroLens collects only the minimum data strictly necessary to fulfill generative media tasks requested by users:

Category of Data Specific Elements Collected Purpose of Collection
Account Identifier Email address and authentication token Authenticating authorized account access and linking existing subscription entitlements.
Generation Directives Task-specific user prompts, duration (seconds), aspect ratio (16:9, 9:16, 1:1), and engine model selection Synthesizing the requested video sequence, ad script, social carousel, or image asset.
Synthesized Outputs Rendered video files, preview images, and generated ad copy Delivering the completed creative asset to the user for download and review.

2. Explicit Exclusion of Restricted & Sensitive Data

ZeroLens strictly does not collect, solicit, store, or process any of the following restricted data categories:

  • Payment Card Data (PCI DSS): We do not collect credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank details within the plugin. All account transactions occur externally on certified payment processors on our main domain.
  • Protected Health Information (PHI): No medical records, health conditions, or biometric health data.
  • Government Identifiers: No Social Security Numbers, Aadhaar, passport numbers, driver's licenses, or national ID numbers.
  • Access Secrets: No plain-text passwords, external API keys, or multi-factor authentication (MFA/OTP) secrets.
  • Sensitive Special Category Data: No racial or ethnic origins, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, sexual orientation, or trade union memberships.

3. Purpose of Use & Processing Scope

All data submitted to ZeroLens is used solely for the explicit purpose of media generation:

  • Processing and rendering user-requested videos, images, and scripts.
  • Tracking task progress via unique generation job IDs.
  • Maintaining account-level credit balances for existing users.
  • Enforcing rate limits and platform integrity to prevent abuse.

We do not use customer inputs or generated assets to train generalized artificial intelligence models without explicit opt-in consent.

4. Categories of Recipients (Third-Party Processors)

To synthesize high-fidelity media, generation parameters are securely routed only to authorized infrastructure subprocessors:

  • Cloud Computing Infrastructure: Secure cloud providers hosting our rendering pipelines and encrypted asset storage.
  • Generative Video/Image Synthesis Engines: Dedicated machine learning inference nodes operating under strict zero-retention service level agreements.

Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal information, generation prompts, or creative media to advertisers, brokers, or unrelated third parties.

5. Data Retention Timelines

We maintain strict retention ceilings to minimize data persistence:

  • Generation Prompts & Parameters: Retained for 30 days in operational cache to allow generation retrieval, after which they are permanently purged.
  • Generated Video & Image Assets: Stored on encrypted cloud storage for 30 days for user download access, after which they are automatically scheduled for permanent deletion.
  • Account Profile Data: Retained for the duration of your active account lifecycle and deleted within 14 business days upon receipt of an account deletion request.

6. Transparency, User Controls & Rights

ZeroLens provides users with full autonomy and control over their data:

  • Right to Access: You may request an export of all generation prompts and assets linked to your account.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request the immediate, permanent deletion of your account, prompts, and synthesized media assets.
  • Pre-Generation Confirmation Control: Within the plugin, no tools are triggered without explicit confirmation of model, duration, and credit cost.
  • No Tracking or Profiling: We do not engage in surveillance, cross-site tracking, keystroke logging, or behavioral profiling.

7. Commerce, Monetization & Transaction Boundaries

In full compliance with OpenAI's Commerce and Monetization Policies:

  • The ZeroLens plugin does not conduct direct commerce, execute checkout flows, or sell digital credits inside the ChatGPT interface.
  • Users may access features included in their existing ZeroLens account.
  • The plugin does not initiate purchase flows, display checkout dialogs, or apply ChatGPT-specific surcharges.
