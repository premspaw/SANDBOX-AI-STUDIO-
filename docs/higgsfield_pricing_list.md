# ZeroLens API Pricing: KIE.AI (Seedance) & Higgsfield (Genjutsu Remix)

> **Official Routing & Margin Policy**:
> 1. **Seedance (Text-to-Video & Image-to-Video)**: Defaulted to **KIE API** pricing as standard. KIE offers identical high-fidelity ByteDance generation at **40–45% lower wholesale cost** than Higgsfield.
> 2. **Genjutsu Motion Transfer & Object Swap (Remix Studio)**: Exclusively routed to **Higgsfield AI (Xfield)** because Genjutsu models are proprietary to Higgsfield and unavailable on KIE API.
> 3. **Platform Margin for Genjutsu**: Set to a competitive **15% Platform Margin Hike** ($Wholesale \times 1.15$) to keep advanced video remixing affordable.
> 4. **Exchange Baseline**: Converted at platform standard rate of **1 USD = ₹95 INR** (1 Short/Credit = ₹1 INR).
