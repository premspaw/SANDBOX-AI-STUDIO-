## 🎬 Video Generation (Veo 3.1)

Pricing is per-second × duration. Default clip = **8 seconds**.

### Veo 3 Fast (1080p) — Most Popular

| Duration | Audio Off | Audio On |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 sec | **93 Shorts** | **140 Shorts** |
| 16 sec | **186 Shorts** | **279 Shorts** |
| 24 sec | **279 Shorts** | **419 Shorts** |

### Veo 3 HQ (1080p) — High Quality

| Duration | Audio Off | Audio On |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 sec | **186 Shorts** | **372 Shorts** |
| 16 sec | **372 Shorts** | **744 Shorts** |

### Veo 3 Fast (4K) — Ultra Premium

| Duration | Audio Off | Audio On |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 sec | **279 Shorts** | **326 Shorts** |

> **Formula:** `ceil(costPerSec × durationSeconds)`
> - Veo Fast 1080p, no audio: ₹11.62/sec → **ceil(11.62 × 8) = 93 Shorts**
> - Veo Fast 1080p, audio: ₹17.43/sec → **ceil(17.43 × 8) = 140 Shorts**
> - Veo HQ 1080p, no audio: ₹23.24/sec → **ceil(23.24 × 8) = 186 Shorts**
> - Veo HQ 1080p, audio: ₹46.48/sec → **ceil(46.48 × 8) = 372 Shorts**
