Why the dark background solves Chrome
Chrome renders favicons on a white tab strip.
A transparent SVG with only lime strokes becomes invisible.

Solution: The favicon has a filled #0a0a0a circle behind the mark.
This creates a self-contained "badge" that reads on any background —
white tab bar, dark mode, pinned tab, bookmarks bar.
